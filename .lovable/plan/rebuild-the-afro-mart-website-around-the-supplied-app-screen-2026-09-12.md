# Rebuild the Afro Mart website around the supplied app screens

## Goal
Make the website a polished gateway to the Afro Mart mobile app: visitors can preview the real app, download it, contact support, or begin the seller journey. The website itself will not present shopping as its main purpose.

## What will change
- Replace the hand-made phone mockup with the supplied Afro Mart app screens and official branding.
- Rework the landing page around three clear actions: download the app, browse as a guest, and start selling.
- Add a focused app showcase using the supplied buyer, service, seller, delivery, chat, and support screens.
- Make guest mode feel like the actual app by switching between supplied Discover, Categories, Services, Store, and Profile screens.
- Keep purchasing, saving, messaging, and booking locked for guests, with a clear sign-in prompt only when those actions are chosen.
- Restyle the seller entry flow to match the supplied seller registration, business onboarding, verification, and dashboard designs.
- Keep Support easy to reach from the header and connect support calls-to-action to the existing support page.
- Remove remaining storefront-style emphasis and any obsolete product-category presentation from the public website.
- Preserve the white, green, gold, and navy visual language shown in the supplied files, including the official logo and correct store badges.

## Technical details
- Store selected uploaded PNG exports through the project asset flow rather than linking to temporary files.
- Use responsive phone frames and image sizing so the screens remain sharp and readable on mobile and desktop.
- Keep the existing browse-only restrictions and authentication gates intact.
- Verify the landing, guest, seller, and support pages on mobile and desktop, including image loading and navigation.
