# Complete the Afromart public website

## Goal
Build the professional public-facing Afromart website shown in the supplied desktop and mobile Figma references. The website introduces the Pan-African ecosystem, promotes the mobile app, supports business accounts and public business profiles, and does not provide a buyer shopping journey.

## Website structure
- **Home:** Recreate the Figma composition and visual language with the headline “Everything Africa. One Marketplace.”, realistic African commerce photography, and a strong app-download focus.
- **About:** Explain Afromart as a Pan-African marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, service providers, local businesses, communities, and cross-border opportunities.
- **Services:** Present tailoring, logistics, repairs, freelance work, and other local services as informational discovery; continue interaction inside the app.
- **For Businesses:** Explain seller benefits and connect signed-in businesses to seller onboarding and management.
- **How It Works:** Discover → Connect → Continue in the App → Grow.
- **Help:** Keep the existing integrated support and AI assistant accessible without presenting buyer order tools as website navigation.
- **Download App:** A focused app page/section using the supplied real app screens and proper App Store and Google Play buttons with placeholder destinations until official links arrive.

## Landing page sections
- Figma-matched header with Home, About, Services, For Businesses, How It Works, Help, account access, language/country controls, and Download App.
- Hero with **Download the App** as the primary action and **Become a Seller** as the secondary action.
- About Afromart and “What you can do” feature categories, with no prices or purchasing controls.
- Services introduction with **Explore Services in the App**.
- Business growth section with **Create Your Business Profile**.
- Trust and safety section using only documented features: seller verification, ratings/reviews, support, secure in-app payments, and dispute handling.
- Pan-African language/localization section covering English, French, Arabic, Portuguese, Swahili, Hausa, Yoruba, Zulu, Amharic, and Igbo.
- App-promotion section with realistic mobile screens and store badges.
- Full footer with working informational and support links.

## Business website experience
- Keep email/Google authentication for seller and business access.
- Keep seller onboarding, store details, logo/banner, location, verification, product/service listing management, settings, orders, and earnings inside the protected business dashboard.
- Create public seller profile pages with business name, logo, banner, location, description, reputation, and informational product/service showcases.
- Public seller profiles will have **View on the Afromart App**, not Add to Cart, Buy, Checkout, or Payment actions.

## Remove from the public website
- Shop, cart, checkout, payment, buyer orders, buyer tracking, product purchase buttons, prices presented for web purchase, and any “Start Shopping”, “Shop Now”, or “Buy Now” CTA.
- Existing buyer-commerce pages will no longer be exposed through website navigation; old public commerce URLs will route visitors to the appropriate app-promotion experience instead of remaining as a parallel web shop.

## Visual implementation
- Match the supplied Figma layout, spacing, green/gold/white palette, Outfit/Figtree typography, compact card styling, photo treatment, and responsive mobile composition.
- Use the screenshot only as a visual reference, not as a page image.
- Reuse the existing realistic Nigerian/African imagery and real uploaded app screens; add no paid/generated media.
- Use “Afromart” consistently throughout.

## Technical details
- Add separate content routes for About, For Businesses, How It Works, Download App, service information, and public business profiles, each with unique metadata.
- Reuse existing seller, verification, support, authentication, language, and storage foundations.
- Keep private seller credentials private and expose only approved business fields publicly.
- Preserve working backend commerce capabilities needed by the mobile app, while removing buyer-commerce presentation and entry points from the website.

## Verification
- Check every navigation item and CTA has a real destination or app-store placeholder action.
- Verify the home, informational pages, support, authentication, seller onboarding/dashboard, and public business profile on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Confirm no public website surface exposes cart, checkout, payment, order tracking, or a purchase action.
- Confirm images load, no page overflows, metadata is unique, authentication remains secure, and the project compiles cleanly.
